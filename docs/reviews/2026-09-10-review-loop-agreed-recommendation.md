## Recommended trial

Apply a finish-by-impact recommendation default to the next three product changes, prospectively from owner adoption. Use the existing artifacts and per-finding owner decisions. The default guides the agents' recommendations; it does not automatically disposition a finding or authorize closure.

1. **Prepare a complete handoff.** Establish the contract and acceptance conditions before implementation. The author validates the completed deliverable against relevant sources and dependencies. A proposed repair is checked against the failing case and valid controls where applicable.
2. **Consolidate independent review.** Codex performs the full first review within its declared scope, covering the behavioral class. Return the findings together. Distinguish older misses, repair regressions and changed requirements when later findings emerge; reconsider reviewer-prescribed remedies too.
3. **Decide by consequence and total cost.** Present the owner with fix-now, defer and accept-residual recommendations per finding, in one concise brief. Include reliance by the next stage, reviewers, owner decisions and safeguards. Recommend closure when required validation is satisfied and remaining risks can be explicitly dispositioned. A recommendation to continue against reviewer closure advice states the delivery benefit against another round's expected cost; a closure recommendation states what the next stage relies on.
4. **Bundle repairs and check their effects.** Aim for one full review and one repair check covering the repair and its dependency radius. Recurrence uses the existing continue / residual / park decision. This is a target, not a review cap. Actual repairs still receive required follow-ups; open findings do not silently become accepted.
5. **Write less without weakening the contract.** Keep decisions, changes, necessary evidence, limitations and pointers in the existing records. Omit speculative root-cause explanations and self-assurance prose from disposition rows and handoffs. Keep authoritative evidence accessible; inspect it when a consequential decision relies on it. Neither location nor keyword searches establish harmlessness.
6. **Evaluate before codifying.** Use existing close-out records to compare elapsed time, owner interventions, repair rounds and consequential escaped defects. After three product changes, decide whether to retain, amend or stop the trial. Do not add a checker, template, ledger or other governance mechanism for this experiment. A result showing fewer reviews alone is insufficient if consequential defects escape.
